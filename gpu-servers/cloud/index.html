Straight answers

FAQ

How does payment actually work for a Russian or Belarusian LLC?

You wire RUB or BYN to a Kyrgyz LLC account. We hold the contract directly with Alibaba Cloud / Tencent Cloud and settle on our side in CNY. Closing documents are issued in your currency, with our Kyrgyz registration on them. No card processors, no offshore wrappers.

How long from invoice paid to SSH?

USDT (TRC-20) — minutes. SWIFT in RUB / CNY — same business day if before 14:00 Moscow, otherwise next morning. We notify you on Telegram the moment funds land, then push credentials within 30–60 minutes.

Can I bring my own container / OS image?

Yes. We ship Ubuntu 22.04 with CUDA 12.4, cuDNN, NCCL and the usual ML stack pre-baked, but you can upload a custom image (Docker, qcow2, ISO) and we'll boot it on bare metal.

What if I need to scale mid-month?

Add SKUs to the cart, generate a top-up invoice — we pro-rate to the existing billing cycle so everything aligns. Downgrades take effect from the next cycle.

Do you do reserved capacity or 12-month contracts?

Yes, on H200 and H100 8× we discount roughly 10% for a 12-month reservation and 18% for 24 months. Email wet4kg@gmail.com with your spec.

Trial or pilot?

After KYC we can give a verified legal entity a 24-hour pilot on a 4090 or A100 node. We don't do free credits for individuals — sorry.
